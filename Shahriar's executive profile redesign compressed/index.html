Profile

Professional Background

Strategy and advisory executive with over 15 years of experience supporting leadership teams, investors, and public-sector stakeholders on transformation and performance improvement across asset-intensive sectors. Work sits at the intersection of strategy, operating-model design, and execution, with a track record advising on large-scale portfolios, complex regulatory environments, and multi-country programs. Known for a pragmatic, data-driven approach to growth, efficiency, and governance challenges, combining senior-level advisory perspective with deep operational understanding to translate strategic intent into measurable outcomes.

Approach

I focus on practical, outcome-driven problem solving, working closely with leadership teams to clarify priorities, structure decisions, and ensure effective execution. My approach combines strategic thinking with operational discipline, ensuring that recommendations are not only well designed, but also realistic, implementable, and measurable. I believe strong results come from aligning strategy with execution, building clear accountability, and creating solutions that deliver sustainable business impact rather than short-term fixes.

Leadership Philosophy

I believe strong leadership is built on clarity, discipline, and sound decision-making. In complex businesses, sustainable success rarely comes from quick fixes or short-term reactions, but from strong foundations, consistent execution, and the ability to make high-quality decisions under pressure. Leadership is not only about setting direction, but about creating trust, alignment, and momentum that allows organizations to scale with confidence.

Track Record

With over 15 years of executive leadership across mobility, transport, and asset-intensive businesses, I have led multi-country operations, managed AED 1B+ asset portfolios, and delivered large-scale transformation across the GCC. My experience spans corporate leadership, investor advisory, public-private stakeholder engagement, and strategic execution across complex operating environments where performance, speed, and decision quality are critical.

I have worked closely with senior executives, investors, founders, and government stakeholders on growth strategy, operating model design, cost transformation, fleet optimization, market expansion, and business scalability. My background combines hands-on operational leadership with strategic advisory, allowing me to bridge the gap between high-level vision and practical execution.

As Vice President of Columbia Business School UAE Chapter and an advisor to GLG and AlphaSights, I support organizations in navigating high-value strategic challenges with clarity, commercial discipline, and measurable outcomes.

I hold an MBA in International Business and a BSc in Industrial Engineering, with executive programmes in General Management and Digital Marketing from Columbia Business School, Six Sigma Green Belt certification, and corporate risk management training from Ernst & Young.
